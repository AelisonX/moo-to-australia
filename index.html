How this works

Cow fiction, honest physics.
  • Goal: land anywhere on Australian land: mainland, Tasmania, islands and external territories (Christmas, Cocos, Norfolk, Heard & McDonald, Lord Howe, Macquarie…). Sea doesn't count.
  • Controls: direction (compass, 0° = north), angle above the horizon, fart power (km/s, relative to the ground). No aim line: learn from your trails.
  • Physics (simplified): round Earth, gravity toward the centre (weaker with distance), Earth spinning the whole time, no air. Your cow already moves with the spinning ground before it farts, so "jump and wait for Australia" does not work: jump straight up and you land a little west.
  • One fart, no second burn: a cow can't accidentally become a satellite. She either comes back down or escapes Earth (that counts as a loss).
  • Fiction: super-fart, heat-proof and crash-proof cow. Don't try this with a real cow.
  • Playback is sped up (shown as ×). "Sim time" is how long the flight lasts in the model; "watched" is real time.
  • The Australian Antarctic claim does not count in this version (pending decision).
